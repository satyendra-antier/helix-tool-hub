# Tool Hub — Business Model & Unit Economics

**Document type:** Revenue model and economics  
**Audience:** Investors, finance, strategic partners

---

## 1. Revenue Model Overview

Tool Hub is **B2B SaaS** sold to technical services firms on an annual or monthly subscription.

| Revenue stream | Type | % of revenue (Y3 est.) |
|----------------|------|------------------------|
| Core platform subscription | Recurring | 85% |
| Domain packs & add-ons | Recurring expansion | 10% |
| Enrichment API credits | Usage-based | 3% |
| Onboarding / GTM workshop | One-time services | 2% |

**Primary metric:** ARR  
**Secondary metrics:** ARPA, NRR, gross margin, CAC payback

## 6. Pricing Philosophy

1. **Value-based, not seat-only** — priced against avoided bad pursuits ($80K+ each) and win rate uplift
2. **Land with strategy, expand to sales** — Growth tier for champion; Scale for rollout
3. **No free tier** — design partner program instead (90-day structured pilot)
4. **Transparent API pass-through** — enrichment credits visible; builds trust
