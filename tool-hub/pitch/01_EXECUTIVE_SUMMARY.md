## One Sentence

Tool Hub is the operating system that tells technical services firms **where to sell**, **whether they can win**, and **who to pursue** — in one closed loop, replacing fragmented market research, capability audits, and lead lists.

---

## The Problem ($40B+ of wasted BD spend)

Technical services firms (blockchain, AI, cybersecurity, enterprise software) lose margin in two predictable ways:

1. **Chasing cold markets** — bidding where demand is weak or commoditized.
2. **Chasing hot markets without depth** — pursuing opportunities they cannot credibly deliver.

The result: low win rates, discounting, delivery risk, and senior architects wasted on unqualified pursuits. Today this is solved with spreadsheets, analysts, and disconnected tools (ZoomInfo + Gartner + manual website audits).

---

## The Solution

Four integrated modules share one database and one dashboard:

| Module | Answers |
|--------|---------|
| **Market Depth Analyzer** | Where is demand growing? (12 live-scored domains) |
| **Build Readiness Engine** | Can we credibly win? (GO / CONDITIONAL / NO_GO with cited proof) |
| **Lead Intelligence** | Who should we pursue now? (demand vs future-oriented pipeline) |
| **Video Scraper** | What proof do we already have? (talks, demos → searchable knowledge) |

**The flywheel:** Market signal → capability proof → qualified lead → revenue — refreshed weekly, not once a year.

---

## Why Now

- **Stablecoin, RWA, and AI agents** are creating new high-margin services categories faster than firms can reposition.
- **LLMs + RAG** make company-specific capability mapping economically viable for mid-market firms.
- **Public signal APIs** (hiring, TVL, GitHub, grants, funding) enable live market scoring without $100K analyst teams.
- Services firms are under pressure to **do more with less BD headcount** post-2023 efficiency cycle.
