## Slide 2 — The Problem

**Services firms bleed margin on the wrong pursuits**

- 📉 **Cold markets** — BD spends months on domains with weak hiring, funding, and deal flow
- 🔥 **Hot markets, no depth** — chasing stablecoin / AI agent deals without proof → low win rate, delivery risk
- 🧩 **Fragmented tools** — market reports + CRM + website audits never connect

**$40B+** spent annually on GTM in technical services; **<20%** of pursuits are systematically qualified.

---

## Slide 3 — The Insight

**Win rate is a data problem, not a hustle problem**

The best firms don't work harder — they align three questions:

1. **Where is demand moving?** (market)
2. **Can we credibly deliver?** (capability)
3. **Who should we pursue?** (pipeline)

Today these are answered by different teams, different tools, different timelines.

---

## Slide 4 — The Solution: Tool Hub

**One operating system. Four modules. One flywheel.**

```
MARKET DEPTH  →  BUILD READINESS  →  LEAD INTELLIGENCE
(where demand)     (can we win?)        (who to pursue)
        ↑                    ↑
        └── VIDEO SCRAPER ───┘
              (proof base)
```

- Live-scored domains (stablecoin, RWA, DeFi, AI agents, LLMOps…)
- GO / CONDITIONAL / NO_GO verdicts with **cited evidence from your website**
- Scored lead pipeline: **demand-oriented** (sell now) vs **future-oriented** (build capability)
