# Tool Hub — Go-to-Market Strategy

**Document type:** GTM plan  
**Audience:** Investors, sales hires, strategic partners

---

## 1. GTM Thesis

Tool Hub sells **revenue focus** to technical services firms — not another dashboard. The GTM motion leads with a **live flywheel demo** on the prospect's own website and market domains, proving value in the first meeting.

**Core message:** *"In 30 minutes, we'll show you where demand is moving, whether you can win, and who to pursue — using your own company as the proof."*

## 5. Sales Process

```
Discovery (30 min)  →  Live demo (45 min)  →  Pilot proposal (90 days)  →  Close  →  Onboard
```

### Discovery call

- Qualify: firm size, practices, current GTM stack, pain on pursuits
- Tease: "We'll index your site and show your GO/NO_GO map live"

### Live demo (the flywheel)

1. **Market Depth** — show their top domains (stablecoin, AI agents, etc.)
2. **Build Readiness** — index their website live (or pre-indexed); show verdicts with citations
3. **Lead Intelligence** — show demand-oriented leads matched to GO domains
4. **ROI close** — "One avoided bad pursuit pays for a year of Tool Hub"

### Pilot structure (90 days)

| Week | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Onboard, index website, configure domains |
| 2–4 | Weekly refresh cadence established |
| 5–8 | Sales uses lead queue; track pursuit decisions |
| 9–12 | Measure: pursuits filtered, leads contacted, win rate proxy |
| 12 | Convert to annual contract or extend |

### Success criteria for pilot → paid

- ≥20% reduction in pursuits in NO_GO domains
- ≥30 qualified demand-oriented leads in pipeline
- Champion rates product ≥8/10 NPS

## 7. Marketing & Category Creation
