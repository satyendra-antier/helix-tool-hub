Most technical services firms don’t lose deals because sales “isn’t hustling enough.”

They lose margin in two predictable ways:

→ **Chasing cold markets** — months of BD in domains where hiring, funding, and deal flow are flat or commoditized.

### Post 5 — Demand vs future-oriented leads
**Status:** Queued  
**Hook:** Unique framework

---

Not every hot market is a “sell now” market.

Some domains are **demand-oriented**: demand is high *and* you can credibly deliver today → pursue now.

Some are **future-oriented**: demand is rising but capability gap is real → invest in positioning, hiring, and proof — don’t fake it in proposals.

Most CRMs and lead tools don’t distinguish the two. They just give you contacts.

Tool Hub tags pipeline orientation from market depth + build readiness together — so you don’t miss growth markets *or* oversell into them.

Simple framework. Hard to run without integrated data.
